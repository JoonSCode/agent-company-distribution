# Find And Use Design References And Skills

Use reference collections, individual examples, installed design skills and
existing tooling proactively when they can inform a named decision: hierarchy,
navigation, typography, feedback, motion, or another experience question. Start
with the current product, platform components and sufficient parent evidence.
Reuse relevant observations instead of repeating their collection; preserve
the observer, date, method, scope and unobserved behavior.

## Resolve The Decision

Name the decision, intended user outcome and what evidence could change the
direction. Search only the gap that remains. A directory helps discover sources;
follow it to concrete examples or original skill documentation. Popularity,
curation and author reputation do not prove product fit or runtime quality.
No fixed source count or broad research round is required for a trivial edit.
Stop when the available evidence supports a bounded choice or exposes a
specific unavailable check.

When interaction or motion is decisive, observe the relevant live behavior
through an authorized path, including interruption, reversal, input and motion
settings where they affect the decision. A screenshot or written case study
alone cannot prove those behaviors. If observation is unavailable, mark that
claim unknown and state the check needed; retain any supported static direction.

Extract the principle and its transfer limits rather than copying the surface.
In the existing decision or handoff record, state **adopt, adapt, or defer** with
the reason, source, applicable platform and verification target. These describe
design reasoning, not dependency or production approval. Tie the choice to the
actual artifact or observed surface when available; a reference link alone
does not prove application. Preserve accessibility and independent final
acceptance under the existing Design contract.

## Choose The Capability

An applicable installed skill can supply a method or tool workflow. Read its
entry instructions and check its scope, required inputs, tool availability and
authority against the current task before use. Use it when those conditions are
already satisfied; ordinary authorized read-only work and reversible role-local
work do not require repeated approval. Preserve explicit skill prerequisites,
including capture or request requirements, and user-selected execution paths.

Discovering a remote skill or repository is different from using an installed
capability. Inspect its original source and scope as read-only evidence; do not
silently install, run, copy source or add a dependency. Use
[component-source-policy.md](component-source-policy.md) for provenance,
licenses, permitted use and adoption ownership instead of creating another
source policy here. A skill's aesthetic advice remains contextual evidence.

For native apps, use platform-appropriate official guidance, resources and
samples for behavior, and applicable installed native design or implementation
skills for technique. Apply [design-knowledge-system.md](design-knowledge-system.md)
and the bound current platform sources for material platform decisions. Website
editorial examples may suggest a hierarchy or emotional direction; they do not
establish universal native navigation, layout or motion rules.

## Conditional Discovery Starting Points

The following sources were recorded in a parent research packet dated
2026-10-08. They are optional discovery leads, not endorsements, a required
roster or current verification. Recheck only what the named decision needs.

- [skills.sh design](https://www.skills.sh/topic/design): a directory; follow
  entries to their original instructions.
- [Anthropic frontend-design](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md),
  [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/) and
  [Emil Kowalski skills](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills): contextual
  design methods and examples. An author's Apple interpretation is not Apple
  official guidance.
- [Awwwards Scrolling](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/scrolling/),
  [Codrops motion roundups](https://tympanus.net/codrops/category/articles/ui-interactions-animations-roundups/)
  and [HOVERSTAT.ES](https://www.hoverstat.es/): collections from which to find
  relevant individual examples and then inspect decisive behavior.

No animation, library, palette, font or editorial structure is required by
these sources. Do not transfer their performance, conversion, quality or
efficiency claims to the current product without corresponding evidence.

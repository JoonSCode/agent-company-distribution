# Optional Decision Lenses

Use only a lens that can change this portfolio choice; skip the rest and reuse
the existing recommendation rather than adding a canvas, journal, or quota.
These are Company adaptations of sources checked in the supplied research on
2026-10-26, not validated prompts or execution authority.

| Lens and source | Use / skip | Benefit, limit, and reversal evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Strategy kernel](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/the-perils-of-bad-strategy) | Use when proposed initiatives conflict or lack a shared diagnosis; skip when the bottleneck and response are already evidenced. | Connect the obstacle, guiding choice and mutually supporting actions. Coherence can still serve a wrong diagnosis; change the recommendation when customer or operating evidence contradicts that obstacle. |
| [Affordable loss](https://effectuation.org/the-five-principles-of-effectuation) | Use when upside forecasts are weak but a bounded learning option exists; skip when a comparable, evidenced forecast already resolves the choice. | Makes downside legible without invented precision. Repeated small losses accumulate, and affordability is not approval or permission to transfer harm; revisit when cumulative exposure, runway or customer risk crosses the approved bound. |
| [Premortem](https://www.gary-klein.com/premortem) | Use before a consequential commitment when plausible failure mechanisms are missing; skip routine reversible choices already covered by relevant failure evidence. | Can expose suppressed dependencies, but imagined disasters are not probabilities. Let a credible early signal or failed containment change the choice; do not expand a speculative risk list into new work. |
| [Reference-class forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.3642) | Use when a material cost or time estimate has genuinely comparable completed cases; omit numerical adjustment without them. | Checks inside-view optimism. Infrastructure findings do not establish app outcomes; revise when the comparison population, actual delivery data or material operating conditions differ. |

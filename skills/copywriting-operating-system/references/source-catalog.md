## Use And Attribution

- Extract a bounded operational rule and link the source.
- Keep direct quotation short and necessary.
- Do not copy an external brand's voice, examples, slogans, or proprietary
  language into product output.
- Treat vendor and practitioner sources as interested evidence.
- Prefer current product and user evidence over a famous method.
- Mark any trend inference as a hypothesis until tested in the target context.

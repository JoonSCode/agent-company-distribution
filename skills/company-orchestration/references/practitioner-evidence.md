# Practitioner evidence

Use reports as hypotheses alongside [model evidence](model-evidence.md) and the
active [routing contract](model-routing.md). A published prompt, role label or
configuration is not proof of runtime compliance, quality or token savings.

[Published task-based dispatch](https://github.com/jvogan/a-fable-of-codexes/blob/main/skills/campaign-conductor/references/codex-dispatch.md)
is one inspectable operating pattern. Read its source and revision before reuse;
it is not a controlled comparison or a reproduced deployment in this package.

## Assess a report

- Match task uncertainty, mistake cost, tools, context and acceptance criteria.
- Retain contrary normal-operation reports, sample limits, harness/model versions
  and observation dates. Mutable pages do not establish publication dates.
- Distinguish deliberate selection, actual applied settings and unintended fallback.
- Compare complete lead/worker/retry/review/integration tokens at accepted quality.
  Delegation may add briefing, polling, context and rework.
- Test a material unresolved hypothesis with a small authorized comparison when
  the expected benefit exceeds its own cost. No paid benchmark or automatic
  fallback permission follows from an anecdote.

Keep personal account observations and decision history in private records.
Current choices come from resolved private configuration or the explicit task,
never from a previous user's narrative in the distributed package.
